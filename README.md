My entry for [A Day of One-Line Pitches](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-a-day-of-one-line-pitches-12cd9bb3) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.py` writes one sentence — at most 240 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. The sentence is a pitch aimed at a friend who has never coded: the app idea they keep describing over coffee could be a real first version on their phone tonight, if they describe it to an AI agent on make.fun in their own words. No code to learn, nothing to lose, and they stay the boss of every change.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
